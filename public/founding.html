IronQ

Lift fordecades,not likes.

IronQ is a strength-training app built by two men in their fifties and sixties who have trained most of their lives. It logs your sets, reads your heart rate from your Apple Watch, times your rest, and stays out of your way.

No feed. No influencers. No streaks to keep up. A workout log that behaves like a good training partner: quiet, exact, and there every session.

Who is building it

John Feitz has trained for roughly fifty years, from Indiana to Orange County, California. He is the co-founder and the person whose training philosophy shaped every screen. You will hear from him directly.

Christian Kameir is a lifter, engineer, and entrepreneur who wrote the first version of IronQ for himself because nothing on the market respected how experienced lifters actually train.

How a session works

  • Pick the exercise, enter weight and repsYour full history for that lift is one glance away, so you always know what to beat.
  • Rest timer runs itselfSet it once per exercise. It counts down without you touching the phone.
  • Heart rate from your wristOpen IronQ on your Apple Watch and your heart rate shows up on the phone during the set.
  • Done means doneEnd the session and the log is saved. Nothing to share, post, or rate.
